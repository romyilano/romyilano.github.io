  2. 02

    Surf or Skate

    Agent-native · Hackathon

    Built in two days at the Agent Native Builders Hackathon at Cloudflare HQ. Answers "surf, skate, or bike?" by sending the question to eight independent agents that argue it out and land on one answer in about a second. Animated fleet diorama, live agent-to-agent stream, self-serve API with no signup.
